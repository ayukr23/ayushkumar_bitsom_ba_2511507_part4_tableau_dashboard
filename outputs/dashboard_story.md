# Dashboard Story: The State of Retail — FY2024 to FY2025
**Audience:** Executive Leadership Team  
**Purpose:** Strategic review and action planning  
**Period:** January 2024 – December 2025

---

## Executive Summary

The business grew in FY2025, but growth is dangerously concentrated. Technology is carrying the entire profit structure (84% of total profit), while Furniture — the second-largest category by sales — is a structural liability. Discounting practices above 20% are actively destroying value. Return rates in Furniture are nearly double the business average. The South region leads in volume, but no region has unlocked a sustainable competitive advantage.

**The headline story: This is a one-category business wearing a three-category suit.**

Unless Furniture is fixed or exited, and Technology's advantage is deliberately expanded, the business faces margin compression as competitors sharpen pricing.

---

## 1. What Is Performing Well

### Technology — The Engine
Technology generates ₹15.39 Cr in sales and ₹2.80 Cr in profit, an 18.2% margin that is 3 points above the business average. Within Technology, four sub-categories — Copiers, Accessories, Phones, and Machines — are all profitable and growing. Same Day and First Class shipping customers, who disproportionately buy Technology, generate the highest per-order profit in the business.

### Home Office Segment — The Silent Winner
Home Office customers spend more per order, return less, and generate slightly better margins than Consumer or Corporate segments. They are underserved relative to their value — there is no dedicated loyalty or account management program for them.

### South Region — Volume Leader
South generates ₹6.47 Cr in sales — 28% more than East — with a comparable margin. The region demonstrates that volume growth is achievable without sacrificing profitability. The sales playbook from South should be studied and replicated.

### Sales Growth Trajectory
Monthly average sales grew from ~₹8.6 Cr in 2024 to ~₹9.3 Cr in 2025, an 8% YoY improvement. The July–August festive cycle shows the business can surge to ₹10.8 Cr per month when conditions align.

---

## 2. What Is Underperforming

### Furniture — A Three-Way Problem
Furniture fails on three dimensions simultaneously:
- **Lowest margin:** 6.9% vs 15.3% business average
- **Highest return rate:** 7.7% vs 4.5% overall
- **Discount dependency:** A disproportionate number of loss-making orders come from Furniture at discounts above 20%

On a fully-loaded cost basis (including reverse logistics for returned items), Furniture may already be margin-negative. This is not a product category in decline — it is a category being badly managed.

### East Region — Volume Gap
East matches West's total sales (₹4.89 Cr each), but both trail South significantly. Given India's Eastern market (West Bengal, Odisha, Jharkhand, Bihar) represents a large addressable base, underperformance here signals a distribution or brand visibility gap, not a market size problem.

### Paid Campaign Channel
The Paid channel generates lower-than-average order values and profit margins compared to Organic and Referral. Marketing spend is not converting into quality revenue. The channel is likely attracting bargain-seeking customers who are more discount-sensitive and have lower retention.

### April Sales Dip
Every April shows a consistent volume dip (~15–20% below the quarterly average). This is a predictable, recurring underperformance — which means it is addressable. The business currently has no counter-seasonal strategy for Q1 end / Q2 start.

## 3. What Risks Are Visible

### Risk 1: Discount Ceiling Breach
Orders with discounts above 30% average a **loss of ₹1,601 per order**. There is no current evidence of a hard discount control in the system. If sales representatives are empowered to offer deep discounts to close deals, the business is trading revenue for volume at a direct cost to the P&L.

**Probability:** High (pattern is visible in current data)  
**Impact:** High (margin destruction at scale)  
**Urgency:** Immediate policy action required

### Risk 2: Furniture Eroding Capital
With 1,140 Furniture orders at 6.9% gross margin and 7.7% return rate, the category is tying up working capital in low-return inventory. If return processing costs (~₹2,000–5,000 per furniture return estimate) are applied to the 88 returns, the effective margin shrinks to near zero or below.

**Probability:** Already occurring  
**Impact:** Medium-term P&L and cash flow drag  
**Urgency:** Category-level P&L audit within 60 days

### Risk 3: Single-Category Concentration
84% of profit coming from one category (Technology) creates brittleness. A supply chain disruption, new competitor, or pricing war in Technology would devastate the business's profitability with no other category capable of compensating.

**Probability:** Medium (Technology markets are competitive)  
**Impact:** Catastrophic if Technology margin compresses even by 5 points  
**Urgency:** Strategic diversification planning within 6 months

---

## 4. What Opportunities Are Visible

### Opportunity 1: Enforce Discount Discipline — Immediate P&L Recovery
If the 20%+ discount policy is enforced, the ~estimated 200–300 loss-making orders per year could be converted to profitable ones. Modeling a conservative recovery to ₹3,000 average profit per order suggests **₹60–90 Lakh annual profit recovery** from policy enforcement alone — with zero investment required.

### Opportunity 2: Home Office Loyalty Program
Home Office customers are the highest-value segment with no dedicated retention effort. A simple loyalty tier, dedicated account manager, or volume discount program for repeat Home Office buyers could increase order frequency by 10–15%, generating ₹70–100 Lakh in incremental sales.

### Opportunity 3: South Playbook → East Expansion
South's volume advantage is real and not margin-destroying. Deploying South's distribution, sales team structure, or campaign mix to East could close the ₹1.6 Cr sales gap between the two regions. Even closing half the gap would add ₹80 Lakh in revenue.

### Opportunity 4: Technology Premium Upsell
Copiers and Accessories have the highest sub-category profit. A structured upsell program — bundling Accessories with Phone or Machine purchases — could increase Technology AOV by 10–15% without requiring new products.

## 6. Limitations of the Dashboard

1. **No customer-level data:** The dashboard analyzes order-level data. Customer lifetime value (LTV), churn rate, and repeat purchase rate cannot be calculated without customer transaction history across orders.

2. **Gross profit only:** The profit field does not include overhead, logistics cost (beyond standard shipping), or reverse logistics for returns. The real P&L impact — especially for Furniture — is likely worse than shown.

3. **Delivery days = ship lead time:** `delivery_days` captures the time between order placement and shipment, not actual delivery to the customer. True last-mile delivery time and customer satisfaction correlation are unknown.

4. **Two-year window:** With only 24 months of data, seasonal trend confidence is limited. A 3–5 year dataset would allow more robust seasonality modeling.

5. **Campaign attribution:** `campaign_channel` has 24 nulls (0.6%) and does not capture multi-touch attribution. A customer acquired via Paid but retained via Email would be attributed to their last or first touch, not the full journey.

## 7. Suggested Next Analysis

1. **Customer cohort analysis** — Track whether customers acquired in 2024 returned to buy in 2025. Cohort retention is the leading indicator of long-term business health.

2. **Furniture sub-category deep-dive** — Isolate Tables and Bookcases with full reverse logistics cost. Determine if specific suppliers or SKUs drive disproportionate returns.

3. **Discount elasticity modeling** — Use regression to estimate the actual price elasticity of demand by category. This would allow the business to set optimal discount levels that maximize revenue × margin, not just apply a blanket ceiling.

4. **RFM Segmentation** — Recency, Frequency, Monetary value segmentation of customers would enable micro-targeted campaigns, improving Paid channel ROI by going after high-RFM customers specifically.

5. **Shipping cost allocation** — Obtain actual shipping cost data per order to calculate true contribution margin by ship mode, category, and region. The current data shows profit before shipping cost allocation.
